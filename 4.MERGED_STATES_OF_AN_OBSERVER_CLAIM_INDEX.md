## Claims

### Def. 2.1, Lem. 2.3

`def:observer` · `lem:basic` · **PROVEN**

Determination and factorization.

**Lean result.** A record determines a target if and only if the target factors through the record, and if and only if the target is invariant under the invisible group; the fibers of the record are the orbits of that group.

**Conditions.** Arbitrary carriers; the decoder is defined on the attained record.

**Source.** [LicenseCore.lean][m1], [MergedPair.lean][m2]

### Thm. 2.5

`thm:boundary-three` · **PROVEN**

The three descriptions of a merged pair.

**Lean result.** Equal records, an invisible permutation exchanging the pair, and zero discrimination of every record-determined reading are equivalent.

**Conditions.** Arbitrary observer and two distinct states; the readings have finite output alphabets.

### Ex. 2.6

`ex:kalman` · **PROVEN**

Unobservable subspace.

**Lean result.** Two states have one output record if and only if their difference lies in the unobservable subspace, the largest invariant subspace in the kernel; observability has a rank criterion.

**Conditions.** The behavioral record of a linear system; finite dimension for the rank statement.

### Thm. 2.7(i),(ii)

`thm:place` · **PROVEN**

Side-alphabet size.

**Lean result.** A resolving coordinate with $n$ symbols exists if and only if every fiber has at most $n$ states; record and coordinate together are bijective.

**Conditions.** Joint injectivity uses fibers of size at most $n$; bijective common-alphabet coordinates require size $n$.

**Source.** [PlaceCoordinates.lean][m6], [PlaceExistence.lean][m7]

### Prop. 3.3

`prop:one-reading` · **PROVEN**

One-reading information.

**Lean result.** The information of one reading is at most its discrimination; under the fair prior it lies between a quarter and a half of the discrimination, and is positive if and only if the discrimination is positive.

**Conditions.** The first bound permits every binary prior; the other three use the fair prior.

**Source.** [BoundarySingularity.lean][m11], [FairReadingBound.lean][m12]

### Thm. 5.4

`thm:pair-resolution-sharp` · **PROVEN**

Repetition attainment.

**Lean result.** Repeated reading reaches the information target at the stated cost, with error bounded by an exponential function of the number of readings; the two-sided price law follows.

**Conditions.** Fair binary label, fixed finite rows, independent repetitions, and unit cost. The positive-discrimination horizon targets $1-h_b(\eta)$ bits.

### Cor. 7.6

`cor:shannon-key` · **PROVEN**

Number of keys and the group cipher.

**Lean result.** Perfect secrecy needs at least as many positive-probability keys as messages, and the group one-time pad attains the bound.

**Conditions.** Finite sets of messages, keys and ciphertexts; a keyed decoder; a fixed law on keys. The lower bound counts positive-probability keys.

**Source.** [BoundarySecrecy.lean][m23], [GroupOneTimePad.lean][m24]
