# A counterexample to Lemma 64 of the Kahrs and Smith proof for RTA-79, and its implications for automated proofs of unique normal forms

Moses Rahnama, Mina Analytics · [Paper: SSRN 7659838](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7659838) · [Lean package](Lemma64-Counterexample/README.md) · [Claim ledger](Lemma64-Counterexample/CLAIMS.csv) · [Standalone repository](https://github.com/MosesRahnama/RTA-79-Proof-Counterexample) · [Repository](README.md)

## The result

Problem 79 of the RTA list of open problems is the conjecture that a term rewriting system has unique normal forms when its rules do not overlap under unification with infinite terms. Kahrs and Smith published a solution, *Non-ω-Overlapping TRSs are UN*, FSCD 2016, [doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2016.22](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2016.22). Their Lemma 64 asserts that every complete targeted proof graph is universal whenever the relation ⇓_A is a consistency invariant.

The paper refutes Lemma 64 with three terms. For the rule `d(x) → x`, the carrier `{c, d(c), d(d(c))}` admits a complete targeted proof graph whose sole parent edge is `d(c) ↦ d(d(c))`. The relation ⇓_A is a consistency invariant on this carrier, and the graph omits the root equation `d(c) ⇓_A c`, so the graph is not universal. The FSCD 2016 proof of the RTA-79 solution obtains transitivity of ⇓ through Lemma 64, so that proof fails at this step. The main theorem of Kahrs and Smith is implemented as a criterion for unique normal forms in the automated provers CSI and ACP; positive answers that these provers justify by the criterion may require an audit, although the counterexample shows no wrong answer.

## The Lean formalization

The package [`Lemma64-Counterexample`](Lemma64-Counterexample/README.md) is one Lean file, [`Lemma64.lean`](Lemma64-Counterexample/Lemma64.lean). It uses Lean 4.22.0-rc4 and Lean's bundled core library, with no Mathlib dependency and no other module of this repository. Every declaration uses only the axioms `propext` and `Quot.sound`. The paper cites the [standalone repository](https://github.com/MosesRahnama/RTA-79-Proof-Counterexample), whose `Lemma64.lean` has the same declarations; the two copies differ only in their header comment.

| Paper result | Lean declaration | Statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theorem 1 | `Lemma64.lemma64_counterexample` | one graph satisfies every hypothesis of Lemma 64 and fails its conclusion |
| Theorem 1 | `Lemma64.lemma64_fails` | the implication of Lemma 64 is false on the carrier |
| Theorem 1, the invariant | `Lemma64.down_universal`, `Lemma64.down_consistencyInvariant` | ⇓_A relates all nine pairs and is a consistency invariant |
| Theorem 1, the graph | `Lemma64.trap_proofGraph`, `Lemma64.trap_targeted`, `Lemma64.trap_complete` | the sole edge gives a proof graph that is targeted and complete |
| Theorem 1, the missing equation | `Lemma64.trap_eqv_exact`, `Lemma64.trap_not_universal` | the graph classes are `{c}` and `{d(c), d(d(c))}`, so `d(c) ⇓_A c` is not represented |
| Section 3, the failed inference | `Lemma64.induction_promotion_fails` | `d(c)` and `d(d(c))` are related by the destructor lifting of ⇓_A and not by that of the graph equivalence |
| Section 3, signature closure | `Lemma64.trap_signatureClosed` | the graph equivalence is signature-closed |

The repair on the same carrier (Section 4) and the dependence of the published proof on Lemma 64 (Section 5) are arguments of the paper, outside the Lean package. [CLAIMS.csv](Lemma64-Counterexample/CLAIMS.csv) lists every node of the proof graph with its source in the paper and in Kahrs and Smith.

## Reproduction

From the `Lemma64-Counterexample` folder, with the pinned toolchain installed, `lake build Lemma64` builds the file and `lake env lean Lemma64.lean` prints each of the 69 audited declarations with its axioms. Building the folder does not build the rest of this repository.
